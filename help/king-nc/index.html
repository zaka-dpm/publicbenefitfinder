Utility Bill Help

City of King Utility Billing and Collections

The City of King bills you for city water and sewer. If you cannot pay your bill on time, call the billing office before the due date. Unpaid bills can get late fees and your service can be shut off. You can pay in person at 229 S. Main Street, online, or by phone at 1-844-778-1892.

Crisis Intervention Program (CIP) — Stokes County DSS

CIP helps if you are in a heating or cooling emergency, like a shut-off notice in winter or summer. It can pay up to $600 per crisis, sent straight to your utility company. You can get help more than once, up to $600 total per state fiscal year.

King Outreach Ministry

King Outreach Ministry helps families in western Stokes County during a crisis. You can get food from their pantry, clothing, help with prescription medicines, and help with energy and heating costs. They can also point you to other agencies that can help.

How to apply →Program details →

Low Income Energy Assistance Program (LIEAP) — Stokes County DSS

LIEAP pays part of your heating bill once a year. The money goes straight to your heat company, not to you. People age 60 or older and disabled people getting DAAS services apply in December. Everyone else applies starting in January, until money runs out or March 31.

Rent & Housing Assistance

Crisis Control Ministry

Crisis Control Ministry helps Stokes and Forsyth County residents in a short-term emergency. They can help pay rent or mortgage, utility bills, and heating fuel. They also run a food pantry where you choose your own food and a free licensed pharmacy. Call to set up a phone or in-person interview.

Food Assistance

East Stokes Outreach Ministry

East Stokes Outreach Ministry serves people in the eastern part of Stokes County, near Walnut Cove. You can get fresh food from their pantry, low-cost clothing and household items from their thrift store, and emergency financial help when times are hard.
