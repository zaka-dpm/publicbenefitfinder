Nebraska Medicaid (Heritage Health)

Last updated August 2026

Nebraska Medicaid is free or low-cost health coverage for children, pregnant people, parents, older adults, people with disabilities, and low-income adults. Most members get care through Heritage Health managed care plans. Nebraska expanded Medicaid, and the Heritage Health Adult program now covers low-income adults 19 to 64. Apply online through iServe Nebraska at iserve.nebraska.gov.
